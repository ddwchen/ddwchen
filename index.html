About

I've always been interested in helping people understand difficult things.

Before marketing, I practiced law. My job was to understand the facts, identify what matters, and build the strongest possible case. Today I do that for companies.

Since then, I've built a travel publication to 600,000+ readers, launched multiple podcasts, built a content agency, and led marketing for startups from pre-seed through Series B.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of strategy, storytelling, and product thinking.
